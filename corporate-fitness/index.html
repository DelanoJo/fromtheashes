For the person organizing it

Let's make the details clear.

You don't need a complete wellness program planned before we talk.

Do we need an on-site gym?

Not necessarily. A movement session may work in an appropriate open room. We'll review the available space and the proposed activities before confirming that the location is suitable.

How many people can join, and how long is a class?

Group size and session length are agreed before booking. They depend on the format, participants, available space, and the level of coaching needed.

What does a group session cost?

Ask for a quote based on your location, group size, session length, and equipment needs. We'll confirm travel and any other charges before you book.

Can people with different fitness backgrounds attend?

We'll discuss the group's experience and appropriate exercise options in advance. Participants can share individual limitations privately with Mia. Some needs may require individual coaching or guidance from a healthcare provider.

Where do you travel for workplace classes?

I'm exploring workplace groups in downtown Denver and Greenwood Village/DTC. Send your address or neighborhood and preferred times so we can discuss whether the location fits my schedule.
